1. Arrive

Environmental ethics protects the territory of agency.

In Chapter 9, you learned that agency needs conditions: breathable air, clean water, stable shelter, health, land, climate, and ecosystems. This workbook helps you scan a decision for the condition at stake, the hidden cost, the distribution of burdens, and a possible repair route.

Privacy reminder: You may use the provided case, a fictional example, a public example, a historical example, a composite example, or a low-stakes personal example. Do not disclose private, identifying, or sensitive information.
2. Read the Case

Cheap Housing, Dirty Air

A city approves a new affordable housing development near an industrial area. The project is praised because housing costs are rising and families need places they can afford. But the area already has poor air quality, truck routes, noise, and pollution. Some families report asthma and breathing problems. Residents and advocates ask whether the cheaper housing is shifting environmental and health costs onto the people with the fewest options.
